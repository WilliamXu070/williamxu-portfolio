# Project evidence sources

These 26 images are derived from original project records: photographs, video frames, application captures, engineering drawings, design notes, geometry plots and a source-code capture. No generated replacement imagery is used. Originals remain intact; web copies are cropped where described, resized and encoded as WebP. Captions distinguish the artifact's stage and the result it supports.

## FRIDAY room asset

The room visible in `friday-room-editor.webp` is **HAUSSMANN APARTMENT** by **Stéphane Agullo (sa3d)**, from [the original SuperSplat scene](https://superspl.at/scene/4de797f4), licensed under [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). The FRIDAY team integrated the scene into its room editor. The portfolio reproduces the team's application screenshot, scaled and re-encoded; the room itself was not captured or authored by William. [FRIDAY's room-source record](https://github.com/LeeSinLiang/FRIDAY/blob/main/docs/frontend/room-capture/haussmann-apartment.md) documents the asset and integration.

The separate checkout frame is cropped from the team's 20 September 2026 recording at approximately 00:25. It shows an accepted Visa IDX sandbox request, not a payment authorization or vendor order. Browser chrome, account information and response identifiers are excluded.

## Interpretation

- CellScope microscopy comparison figures retain their raw/reconstructed/reference distinctions. The tabletop microscope reference is a separate instrument. Acquisition time is distinct from reconstruction latency.
- Photonics figures include archived configurations. Geometry visualizations and reference reconstructions are not measured device performance or proof of transmission improvement.
- HackerFab captures are native OpticStudio design studies. The objective is a published-prescription proxy, and the preserved-spacing exposure check failed; absent edge-field spots do not indicate zero aberration.
- HALO shows the actual model-loader implementation at a pinned commit. It does not substitute for a phone demo, hardware latency measurement or clinical validation. Clinical data integration uses a demo FHIR server.
- WaferWatch is an earlier working visualizer. A current process/history capture remains outstanding.
- Bridge drawings and test footage are team work. The 1.367 kN figure in the narrative is the design prediction, not a load read from the video.
- Looky images show imported test fixtures. They do not establish original authorship of the imported parts or validate selection accuracy by themselves.
- Can-opening material is team concept-development evidence, not a manufacturing-ready product or an independently validated sound-reduction claim.
