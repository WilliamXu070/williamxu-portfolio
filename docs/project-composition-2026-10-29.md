# Project composition — 29 October 2026

## Design brief

Preserve the full-screen FSD opening. Recompose the projects into an editorial gallery that makes the physical and software work legible at a glance. The audience is an engineering reviewer looking for the system, William's role, and evidence that it exists.

References: Gonzalo Morales (https://gmoral.es/) for unequal project scale and staggered placement; Christine Liang (https://www.christineliangdesign.com/) for image-first presentation and concise contribution captions. Retain the established Jane Koo / Tiffany Tedy influences in the restrained opening and interactive case studies. Reference designers' assets are not copied.

## Tokens and layout

- Existing Helvetica Neue / Arial, paper #f5f5f3, ink #171a20, secondary #62666d.
- Maximum project width 1376 px; desktop gutters 72 px; mobile gutters 20 px.
- Five prominent projects: CellScope / photonics in a staggered 7:4 pair, full-width FRIDAY, WaferWatch / HackerFab in a second offset pair.
- Full original artifacts, quiet captions outside the image, large regular-weight titles. Image-hover labels have keyboard equivalents. No enclosing dashboard panels or repeated feature-card grid.
- The remaining 11 projects form a compact, two-column archive with genuine thumbnails where evidence exists. All 16 case studies remain accessible.
- On mobile, pairs stack with slight width variation and consistent reading order. No scroll interception, motion library, or additional hero animation.

## Evidence and accessibility review

The CellScope photograph remains uncropped apart from its near-identical native aspect ratio. Technical diagrams and application screenshots use contain. Case-study prose, results, source credits and detailed inspection controls stay intact. FRIDAY's licensed room, WaferWatch's earlier prototype, and the photonics / Zemax study limitations remain explicit.

Keep existing FSD pause/play, reduced-motion behavior and fallback. Use semantic headings and native links. Focus indication remains visible; information does not depend on hover. Check 1440 px, 390 px and 320 px layouts, image loading, all project routes and a sample case-study interaction before committing.
